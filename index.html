Geolocation Spoofing Heuristic Checker

This browser-only demo requests location access, collects a stream of readings, and estimates spoof likelihood by checking consistency over time.

Results are probabilistic, not definitive proof. Browsers do not expose a trusted "mock GPS" flag, so this tool only estimates risk from behavioral inconsistencies.
